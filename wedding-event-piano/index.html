Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as you have a date and venue, ideally several months out, since weekend dates fill first. That said, reach out even if your date is close. It’s always worth checking availability.

Can you learn a specific song?

Yes. Send the piece, or a recording if there’s no sheet music, as early as you can, and I’ll learn it for your event.

What happens if the venue has no piano?

I bring a keyboard sized and voiced for the room. A venue’s own piano is welcome when there is one, but it isn’t required.

Can you play an outdoor ceremony?

Outdoor ceremonies work when the ceremony space is covered, such as a tent or covered patio, and the venue provides its own sound system. Both conditions need to be true for an acoustic instrument to hold up outside.

Ready to book your date?

Tell me your date, venue, and what you’re picturing for the music, and I’ll follow up with next steps.
